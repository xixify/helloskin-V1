    Assumptions in this preview

    1. All prices are placeholders in BDT, set from typical Bangladesh retail for each brand and size. Your sheet has no prices. Each product page marks its price as a placeholder.
    2. Product names, categories and brands come from your sheet, with the Phase 1 corrections: the 16% Vitamin C + E + Ferulic serum is Minimalist, the Lip Treatment Balm is Minimalist L-Ascorbic Acid, and the Scalp Serum Roll-On is Bodywise.
    3. Photos are your real files, reassigned to the correct product after the Phase 1 check. Up to 4 per product are shown, resized for the web. Strawberry Dew Spotlight Strobe Cream has no photo, so it shows a styled placeholder.
    4. Cards use a 1:1 frame, not 4:5. Your photos are square ad creatives, and a 4:5 crop would cut off their text. Clean packshots would let us switch to 4:5.
    5. Key ingredients and How to use are based on the one-line description in the sheet, plus general usage for the product type. They are marked “Draft copy” and need the real details from each brand.
    6. Delivery and returns terms are examples: ৳60 inside Dhaka, ৳120 outside, free over ৳2,000, and 7-day returns on unopened items. Ratings and review counts are also placeholders.
    7. Bestsellers are a hand-picked set of 8 products with strong photo sets. Tell me your real top sellers.
    8. Your reference sites (Soko Glam, SkinKraft, Purplle) shaped three retail touches on top of the minimal base: a shop-by-brand strip, star ratings on product cards, and a free-delivery progress bar in the bag.
    9. Checkout, account, search results pages and the newsletter are not connected in this preview. The cart, filters, sorting and quick search all work.
